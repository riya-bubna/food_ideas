## Ingredients

Makes 2 tacos — 1 serving.

### Mango salsa
- 33 g mango, cubed
- 33 g tomatoes, cubed
- 17 g red onion, diced
- 17 g jalapeño, diced
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- Salt to taste

### Tofu
- 170 g firm high-protein tofu
- 30 ml low-sodium soy sauce
- 5 g spicy taco seasoning
- Olive oil spray

### To assemble
- 2 thin corn tortillas
- Cilantro, chopped
- Lime juice

## Instructions

1. Wash and prep all the salsa vegetables. Combine in a bowl and mix so the
   flavours marinate together.
2. Add the lime juice and salt to taste. Mix and adjust.
3. Grate the tofu into a bowl.
4. Season with the soy sauce and taco seasoning. Mix thoroughly.
5. Heat a pan over medium with olive oil spray. Add the shredded tofu and cook
   8–10 minutes, until golden brown and firmed up.
6. Warm the corn tortillas briefly in a pan as the tofu finishes.
7. Build the tacos: tofu into each tortilla, topped with the mango salsa.
8. Finish with fresh cilantro and lime juice.

## Notes

Claude: the creator published macros for this one — 431 kcal, 33 g protein,
44 g carbs, 11 g fat per serving. That's her figure, not my estimate, and it's what
`high-protein` is tagged from here.

Claude: the reel's caption lists ingredients with no quantities and gates the real
amounts behind commenting "RECIPE". The quantities above came from her website
instead, so nothing was posted from your account.

Claude: grating rather than crumbling the tofu is the whole point — the shreds
crisp at the edges and take on a mince-like texture. A box grater on the coarse
side works.

Claude: don't crowd the pan at step 5. Shredded tofu steams instead of browning if
it's piled up, and you lose the texture the recipe is built around.

Claude: quantities are sized for a single serving and are unusually precise
(33 g mango). Scale up freely — round to a small handful each and it's fine.

Claude: low-sodium soy sauce usually contains wheat, so this isn't gluten-free as
written despite the corn tortillas. Swap in tamari if that matters.
