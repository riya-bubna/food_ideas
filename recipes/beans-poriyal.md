**Source:** [Beans Poriyal, Tarla Dalal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NGiN9vscfU4)

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil, or any refined oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp urad dal
- 1 tsp chana dal
- 1 dry pandi chilli, broken into pieces
- 1 tsp green chillies, finely chopped
- 1/4 tsp asafoetida
- 1/4 cup onions, finely chopped
- 2 1/2 cups french beans, chopped
- Salt to taste
- 6–7 curry leaves
- 1/4 cup freshly grated coconut

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a kadhai and add the mustard seeds.
2. When the seeds crackle, add the urad dal, chana dal, pandi chilli, green
   chillies, asafoetida and onions. Sauté on a medium flame for 2 minutes, stirring
   continuously.
3. Add the french beans, salt and 1/4 cup water. Mix well, cover, and cook on a
   medium flame for 8–10 minutes, until cooked through.
4. Add the curry leaves and coconut, mix gently, and cook another 2 minutes.
5. Serve hot.

## Source notes

Tarla Dalal's own tips:

- Desiccated coconut works if you don't have freshly grated.
- Swap the beans for cabbage to make cabbage poriyal.
- Turmeric powder can be added if you like.

## Notes

Claude: the dals are a tempering ingredient, not a pulse to cook through — they go
in at step 2 to toast and go nutty and crunchy. Two minutes of continuous stirring
is what stops them burning.

Claude: 1/4 cup of water and a lid is all the beans get. Don't add more, or you'll
steam them into a wet sabzi instead of a dry poriyal.

Claude: coconut and curry leaves go in at the very end and cook only 2 minutes.
Adding them earlier loses the fresh coconut flavour that defines the dish.

Claude: not tagged gluten-free — compounded asafoetida is usually cut with wheat
flour. Check yours if it matters.

Claude: about 3 g protein per serving. This is a side dish to go with rice or roti,
not something to build a meal around.

Claude: your first South Indian recipe and your first dry sabzi. Useful alongside
the soups and salads when you want something small and hot rather than cold.
