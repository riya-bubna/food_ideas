## Ingredients

- 1 large potato
- Skewers
- Cold water, for soaking
- 1–1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, to taste
- Kashmiri red chilli powder, to taste
- Mixed herbs, to taste

## Instructions

1. Wash and peel the potato (peeling is optional). Push a skewer through the centre
   and spiral-cut it with a slicer.
2. Soak the spiral potato in cold water for 20 minutes to draw out excess starch.
3. Drain well and pat completely dry — this step is the one that decides whether it
   crisps.
4. Mix the olive oil, salt, Kashmiri red chilli powder and mixed herbs in a bowl.
5. Brush the oil mixture evenly over the dry spiral potato.
6. Preheat the air fryer to 170°C.
7. Air fry at 170°C for 12–15 minutes, turning once midway, until cooked through and
   evenly crisp.

## Source notes

From the creator: if the edges brown too fast while the centre is still undercooked,
drop to 165°C and air fry another 2–3 minutes.

## Notes

Claude: steps 2 and 3 are the whole recipe. The soak pulls out surface starch and the
drying lets the oil actually contact the potato — skip either and you get a limp
spiral rather than a crisp one.

Claude: spread the spiral open along the skewer before it goes in, so hot air reaches
every face. Left compressed it steams in its own gaps.

Claude: one potato is a snack for two at most. It scales by simply doing more
skewers, but they need space in the basket, so cook in batches rather than crowding.

Claude: about 3 g protein. This is a snack for a dip, not something to build on.

Claude: pairs naturally with the [muhammara](muhammara-dip.md) — you already eat that
with sweet potato air fries, and this is the same idea with a spiral cut. Sweet potato
works here too, though it softens faster, so check at 10 minutes.
