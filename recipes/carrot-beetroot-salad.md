**Source:** [@snehasinghi1 on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CqSEMLBgAc4/)

The creator learnt it from her mother, who learnt it at Isha. She sometimes eats it
as a meal with a bowl of curd.

## Ingredients

Serves 4 as a side.

### Salad
- 1 cup carrot, grated
- 1 cup beetroot, grated
- 1/4 cup coconut, shredded
- 1/4 cup peanuts
- 1/4 cup coriander leaves
- Black salt to taste

### Tempering
- 1 tbsp olive or refined oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 2 dried red chillies
- 8–10 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp urad dal
- 1 tbsp chana dal

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine the grated carrot, beetroot and shredded coconut.
2. Coarsely crush the peanuts and add them, with the coriander leaves and black salt.
3. Heat the oil in a pan. Add the mustard seeds, dried red chillies, curry leaves,
   urad dal and chana dal. Cook 30–40 seconds, until the dals brown slightly.
4. Pour the tempering over the salad and mix well.
5. Serve as a side, or on its own.

## Notes

Claude: the tempering is the same technique as [beans poriyal](beans-poriyal.md) —
mustard seeds, urad dal, chana dal and curry leaves bloomed in hot oil. Learn it once
and it works over almost any raw or steamed vegetable. That's probably the most
reusable thing in this file.

Claude: the dals need to actually brown at step 3, not just warm through. A commenter
found them hard to chew, which is what happens if the tempering comes off the heat too
early — they're meant to go crunchy, not stay raw.

Claude: pour the tempering over while it's still hot. It's what wilts the coconut
slightly and carries the aroma into the raw vegetables.

Claude: **several commenters independently recommend adding lemon juice**, and the
creator doesn't include any. Given beetroot's earthiness and the absence of acid in
the ingredient list, that's worth trying — a squeeze at step 4.

Claude: about 4 g protein per serving, so this is a side. The creator's own suggestion
of eating it with a bowl of curd is what turns it into a light meal.

Claude: grate the beetroot last and mix quickly, or everything turns uniformly pink.
Some people prefer that; the video keeps the colours distinct.

Claude: another option for a raw carrot salad, with an entirely different accent:
the [sesame ginger carrot salad](sesame-ginger-carrot-salad.md) — ribbons rather than
grated, soy and ginger rather than coconut and tempering.
