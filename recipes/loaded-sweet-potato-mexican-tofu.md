## Ingredients

- 2 large sweet potatoes
- 1 pack Soyco high-protein Mexican Scramble Tofu
- 1/4 cup crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 tbsp finely chopped fresh coriander, divided
- 1/2 lime
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1 large tomato, diced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Dairy-free sour cream or plain yogurt, to top
- Olive oil and salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Cut the sweet potatoes in half lengthways and coat them with oil and salt. Place
   them cut-side down on a lined tray.
3. Prick the skins 2–3 times and bake for 30–40 minutes, until soft.
4. Heat the scramble tofu in a non-stick pan. Mix in the crushed tomatoes and tomato
   paste and cook until warmed through, then stir in some of the coriander.
5. In a bowl, combine the red onion, tomato, the juice of the lime half and the rest
   of the coriander.
6. Flip the cooked potatoes and fluff the centres with a fork.
7. Top with the tofu mixture, the tomato-onion salsa, avocado, sour cream or yogurt,
   and a pinch of salt.

## Notes

Claude: I took this from rung 1, the video's caption, which had the full recipe. The
caption doesn't say how many it serves. Two large potatoes reads as 2 servings (two
halves each), which suits your household.

Claude: **air fryer instead of the oven.** Put the halves cut-side down at 185 °C and
start checking at 25 minutes. They're done when a knife slides through. Four halves
of large potatoes usually fit in one basket in a single layer. If they overlap, cook
in two batches. The microwave also works if you're short on time: 6–8 minutes until
soft, then 8–10 minutes in the air fryer to crisp the cut faces.

Claude: **Soyco is an Australian brand, so you probably can't buy it.** To make your
own, crumble 250–300 g firm tofu into a hot oiled pan with 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked
paprika, 1/2 tsp chilli powder, 1/2 tsp garlic powder and a pinch of turmeric, then
carry on from step 4. I'm guessing at the pack size. Soyco's plain scrambled tofu
comes in 300 g packs, and the Mexican one is likely the same.

Claude: tomato puree or passata works for the crushed tomatoes. Use hung curd or Greek
yogurt for the topping and it's no longer vegan, but you get more protein.

Claude: **the "high-protein" tag assumes about 150 g of tofu per person.** With
paneer instead, crumbled and cooked the same way, you get a version that's closer to
