## Ingredients

Makes 4 jars. Keeps 4–5 days.

- 1.5 cups rajma, boiled
- 100 g paneer, in cubes
- 1.5 cups sweet corn, boiled
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Peri peri seasoning — amount not specified
- Smoked sea salt, to taste
- 1/2 cup curd (yogurt)
- 2 tbsp coriander, chopped
- 1 small lime
- 1 cup cucumber, chopped
- 1 cup tomatoes, chopped

## Instructions

1. Boil the rajma and mash gently. Sauté in olive oil with peri peri and smoked sea
   salt. Add the paneer cubes and a splash of water, and cook.
2. Boil the sweet corn, then air fry with peri peri, salt and a little olive oil.
3. In a bowl, mix the curd, chopped coriander and lime juice.
4. Add the chopped cucumber and tomatoes.
5. Fold in the rajma-paneer prep and the crispy corn. Mix.
6. Divide into jars, or eat right away.
