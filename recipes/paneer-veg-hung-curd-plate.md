**Source:** My own — no external recipe.

> **A three-component plate, not a fixed recipe.** Grilled paneer, stir-fried
> vegetables, herbed hung curd. Each part swaps freely.

## Ingredients

Cooked by eye; no quantities recorded.

### Grilled paneer
- Paneer, cubed
- Black pepper, coarsely cracked
- Salt
- Oil, for the pan

### Stir-fried vegetables
- Bell peppers — green and yellow
- Carrot
- Onion

### Hung curd dip
- Hung curd
- Fresh herbs, finely chopped
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Cube the paneer and season generously with cracked black pepper and salt.
2. Grill or pan-fry until golden on several sides. Set aside.
3. Cut the peppers, carrot and onion into large pieces and stir-fry over high heat
   until they take colour but stay crunchy.
4. Stir the chopped herbs and salt through the hung curd.
5. Plate the vegetables, pile the paneer alongside, and spoon the hung curd dip into
   the middle.
