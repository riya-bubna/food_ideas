## Ingredients

Makes 6–7 tacos.

### Beans
- 1/2 cup rajma, soaked overnight
- 1.5 cups water
- Salt
- Lime juice

### Salsa
- 1 medium onion, small diced
- 2 tomatoes, small diced
- 1–2 spicy green chillies, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp coriander, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- Chilli lime seasoning
- 1/2 tsp Tabasco
- Salt to taste

### To assemble
- 6–7 small rotis (whole wheat or all-purpose)
- 1 cup mozzarella, grated
- Chipotle mayo
- Oil, for the pan

## Instructions

1. Soak the rajma overnight. Pressure cook with 1.5 cups water and salt for 8–9
   whistles, until very soft.
2. For the salsa, combine the onion, tomato, green chillies, coriander, salt,
   chilli lime seasoning and lime juice.
3. Mash the rajma with salt and lime juice, adding 1–2 tbsp of the cooking liquid,
   until soft.
4. Heat the rotis in a pan with a little oil.
5. Spread the mashed rajma over one half, add the grated cheese, and fold the roti
   over.
6. Cook on both sides until golden brown.
7. Open the taco just before serving, add the salsa and chipotle mayo.
8. Serve hot.

## Notes

Claude: reserve the rajma cooking liquid before draining — step 3 needs it, and
it's easy to tip down the sink at step 1.

Claude: the salsa goes in *after* frying, not before. Folding it in raw releases
water and the roti steams instead of crisping.

Claude: lands around 23 g protein per serving, clearing the 20 g bar. Extra cheese or a
bigger bean portion pushes it further if you want.

Claude: this is the closest thing in your collection to a genuine weeknight
crowd-pleaser. The rajma can be cooked and mashed a day ahead, which drops the
day-of work to about 15 minutes.
