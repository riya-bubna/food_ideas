## Ingredients

Serves 3–4 (makes 15–16).

### Kebabs
- 150 g soya granules
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 medium red bell pepper, finely chopped
- 2–3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 inch ginger, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- Handful of fresh coriander leaves, chopped
- Salt to taste
- 1 tsp roasted cumin powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon powder
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 1 cup besan (gram flour)
- 450 ml water
- Oil for brushing, amount not specified (used in the video, not listed)

### Yogurt dip
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 1 small cucumber, grated, water squeezed out
- Salt to taste
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan. Add the soya granules with the dry
   spices, salt and soy sauce.
2. Mix well, cover, and cook 5–6 minutes until all the water is absorbed.
3. Let it cool to room temperature. Mix in the onion, ginger, garlic, coriander, red
   bell pepper and the besan.
4. Mix everything well and rest 10 minutes.
5. Shape the mix into kebabs around skewers.
6. Toast on a flat pan or tawa, brushing with oil and turning often so every side
   cooks.
7. For the dip, mix the yogurt, squeezed cucumber, salt and black pepper.
8. Serve the kebabs warm with the dip.
