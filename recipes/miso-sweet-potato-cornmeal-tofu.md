## Ingredients

### Whipped sweet potato
- 2 lb sweet potatoes (about 3 medium), scrubbed and pierced with a fork
- 1 small bulb garlic, cut to expose the cloves
- Avocado oil, for roasting
- 1 tbsp yellow miso paste
- 2 tsp maple syrup
- 1/4–1/2 cup vegetable broth

### Tofu
- 1 lb block extra-firm tofu, drained and pressed
- Juice of half a large lemon
- 1 1/2 tbsp tamari
- 1 1/2 tbsp avocado oil
- 2 tbsp cornmeal
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp fresh thyme

### Marinated kale (optional)
- 12 kale leaves, stems removed and thinly sliced
- 3 spring onions, thinly sliced
- Juice of half a large lemon
- 2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Kosher salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Halve the sweet potatoes lengthwise and put them on a
   tray with the garlic bulb. Drizzle with oil, then turn both cut-side down.
2. Roast 30–40 minutes, until the potatoes are caramelised underneath and soft.
3. Tear the tofu into 1-inch chunks. Put in a large container with the lemon juice
   and tamari, seal, shake, and leave 5 minutes.
4. Add the oil, cornstarch, cornmeal and seasonings. Seal and shake again to coat
   evenly.
5. Spread the tofu on a tray with space between the pieces. Bake 20 minutes, flip,
   and bake 10 minutes more.
6. Combine the kale, spring onions, lemon, oil, maple syrup, mustard and a generous
   pinch of salt. Massage into the kale until softened, then cover and refrigerate
   until serving.
7. Once cool enough to handle, scoop the sweet potato flesh and squeeze the roasted
   garlic into a food processor with the miso, maple syrup, 1/4 cup broth and a
   pinch of salt. Process on high, scraping down, until smooth. Add more broth to
   loosen if you want.
8. Smear the sweet potato onto a plate, top with the kale, then the tofu.

## Notes

Claude: about 25 g protein per serving from a full pound of tofu — one of only a
handful of high-protein mains here, and the only vegan one that isn't a taco or a
bowl.

Claude: tearing rather than cubing the tofu (step 3) is deliberate, same principle as
the [sticky lemon tofu](sticky-lemon-garlic-tofu.md) — ragged edges hold the cornmeal
and crisp properly.

Claude: shaking in a sealed container rather than tossing in a bowl is what gets an
even coat without knocking the crust off. Worth following literally.

Claude: cut-side down at step 1 is what caramelises the sweet potato. Face up, it
just bakes, and the whipped base loses the roasted depth the miso is playing against.

Claude: the marinated kale uses exactly the massaging technique worth applying to
your [kale salad](kale-fruit-feta-salad.md) — lemon, oil, salt, worked in by hand
until the leaves soften. A commenter who disliked kale's bitterness said this
marinade changed her mind.

Claude: all three components hold separately. The sweet potato and kale can be done a
day ahead; only the tofu wants to be fresh from the oven.

Claude: miso is the whole point of the base — it makes a sweet vegetable savoury. No
substitute really works, so don't make this without it.

Claude: the [miso glazed tofu](miso-glazed-tofu.md) uses the same tub of white miso
for a fraction of the effort — worth knowing when you've opened one for this.
