**Source:** [@amandaducks on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DLwlZhyp4jW/)

Written for 2 servings — no scaling needed.

## Ingredients

- 450 g firm tofu
- 1 tbsp corn flour
- 2 tsp vegetable stock powder (or vegan chicken stock powder)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/8 cup maple syrup
- 4 garlic cloves, grated or crushed
- 2 servings of stir-fry greens — broccoli, green beans or pak choy

### To serve
- Cooked rice
- Spring onions
- Fresh chilli
- Sesame seeds
- Dried shallots

## Instructions

1. Tear the tofu into chunks. Add to a bowl with the corn flour and stock powder
   and toss to coat well.
2. Heat a frying pan over medium-high with the olive oil. Once hot, add the tofu
   and fry for 15 minutes, tossing every few minutes.
3. Meanwhile, mix the lemon zest, lemon juice, soy sauce, maple syrup and crushed
   garlic in a bowl.
4. Wash and cut your greens.
5. Add the greens to the pan and fry 2–3 minutes. Reduce the heat to low, add the
   sauce, and fry, stirring continuously, for a further 15 minutes. Remove from
   the heat.
6. Serve hot with rice and the garnishes.

## Notes

Claude: *tearing* the tofu rather than cubing it is deliberate — the ragged edges
catch the cornflour and crisp, which is what makes the sauce cling.

Claude: 30 minutes of total pan time (15 + 15) is much longer than most tofu
recipes and it is not a typo in the caption. The second 15 minutes is what reduces
the sauce to sticky. Cutting it short leaves you with watery glaze.

Claude: stir continuously at step 5. Maple syrup plus soy reduces fast and will
catch on the pan base once it thickens.

Claude: around 36 g protein per serving from 450 g of tofu — comfortably the
highest-protein recipe in your collection, and it already serves 2.

Claude: press the tofu first if yours is packed in water. The recipe doesn't say
to, but wet tofu won't take the cornflour coating at step 1.

Claude: if you want the same one-pan glazed tofu with a savoury-sweet Japanese
accent instead of a citrus one, the [miso glazed tofu](miso-glazed-tofu.md) is the
swap — and it has a source-provided air fryer method, which this one doesn't.
