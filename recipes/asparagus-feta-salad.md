## Ingredients

Serves 4–6.

### Asparagus
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb (about 450 g) asparagus, tough ends removed, cut into 3–4 pieces
- 1/2 tsp lemon zest (about 1/3 of a lemon)
- 2 large garlic cloves, finely chopped
- A pinch of salt

### Salad
- 10 oz (about 300 g) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- 3 oz (about 90 g) feta, crumbled
- A few sprigs of fresh dill

### Dressing
- 1.5 tbsp lemon juice
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp honey
- 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/4 tsp salt
- A pinch of black pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the asparagus and
   lemon zest and cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the garlic and cook a further 1–2 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the
   asparagus is bright green and slightly charred. Remove to a salad bowl and let
   cool.
3. While it cools, halve the tomatoes and slice the red onion.
4. Whisk the dressing ingredients together in a small bowl.
5. Add the tomatoes, onion, crumbled feta and dill to the asparagus. Pour over the
   dressing and toss gently. Serve at room temperature or chilled.

## Notes

Claude: the asparagus gets 2–3 minutes total, and that's the point — it should stay
snappy. Cooking it to tender turns this into a limp warm salad.

Claude: garlic goes in *after* the first minute, not with the oil. Two minutes is
all it can take at that heat before it burns and turns bitter.

Claude: let the asparagus cool properly before step 5. Added warm, it wilts the
dill and softens the feta into paste.

Claude: honey and Dijon are what hold the dressing together — the mustard emulsifies
it. Skipping the Dijon gives you oil and lemon that separate on the plate.

Claude: about 5 g protein per serving. This is a side, not a main — pair it with
something from the tofu recipes if you want it to be dinner.
