## Ingredients

### To roast
- 2 red peppers
- 2 tomatoes
- 1 onion
- 1 whole head garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper

### For the sauce
- 1/3 cup walnuts
- 2–3 tbsp breadcrumbs
- 2–3 tsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp anar dana powder (pomegranate seed powder)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste (optional)
- 2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
- A little water, to loosen

### Caramelized onion
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, sliced
- Pinch of sugar
- Pinch of salt

### To assemble
- 400 g pasta of choice
- 2 tbsp oil
- Chilli flakes
- Fresh basil

## Instructions

1. Toss the red peppers, tomatoes, onion and whole garlic head with olive oil,
   salt and pepper. Roast in a pre-heated oven at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, until
   charred.
2. Dry-roast the walnuts separately and set aside.
3. Blend the roasted vegetables with the walnuts, breadcrumbs, lemon juice, anar
   dana powder, tomato paste, Kashmiri chilli powder and a little water to a
   purée.
4. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a pan over low heat. Add the sliced onion with a
   pinch each of sugar and salt and cook slowly until caramelized.
5. Boil the pasta. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
6. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan and cook the sauce for 2 minutes. Add the pasta and
   splashes of pasta water until the consistency is right. Add chilli flakes and
   toss.
7. Garnish with basil, the caramelized onion, and more chilli flakes.

## Notes

Claude: anar dana powder carries the tart, fruity backbone of muhammara. If you
don't have it, substitute 1–2 tbsp pomegranate molasses and pull back slightly on
the lemon.

Claude: the walnut-to-vegetable ratio is low for a traditional muhammara, so this
reads more like a smoky red pepper sauce than a nut-forward one. Increase the
walnuts to 1/2–3/4 cup for something closer to the original.

Claude: squeeze the roasted garlic out of its skins before blending — the recipe
doesn't say so, but you don't want the papery husks in the sauce.

Claude: the sauce freezes well. Worth making a double batch of steps 1–3 and
freezing half.

Claude: despite the walnuts this is not a high-protein dish — 400 g of pasta
across four servings makes it firmly carb-led. If you want it to land as
high-protein, swap in chickpea or lentil pasta, or stir 250 g of crumbled paneer
through at step 7. The paneer suits the smoky sauce.
