**Source:** [@snehasinghi1 on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DTFCQfbk1Wh/)

Written for 2 servings — no scaling needed.

## Ingredients

- 1 tsp oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 7–8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, finely chopped
- 200 g tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp vinegar
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- Salt to taste
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1/4 cup water
- 8–10 fresh basil leaves
- A handful of spring onion greens, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp white sesame seeds, toasted

## Instructions

1. Spray or brush oil in a frying pan. Add the onion, garlic and ginger and sauté 30
   seconds.
2. Add the tofu and cook until golden brown on both sides.
3. Add the soy sauce, vinegar, chilli flakes, salt, pepper and water. Cook until the
   water has evaporated.
4. Toss in the basil leaves and spring onion greens.
5. Mix in the sesame seeds and serve hot, with steamed rice or on its own.

## Notes

Claude: 7–8 garlic cloves against 200 g of tofu is a lot, and it's finely chopped
rather than crushed, so it goes in for only 30 seconds before the tofu. Watch it —
that quantity burns fast and there's nothing else here to hide it.

Claude: the 1/4 cup water at step 3 is a deglaze, not a sauce. Cooking it off is what
concentrates the soy and vinegar onto the tofu rather than leaving it wet.

Claude: basil at the very end, off the heat if possible. It's the defining flavour
and it turns black and bitter if it cooks.

Claude: about 17 g protein per serving — noticeably lower than your other tofu mains,
because this uses 200 g for two where the
[sticky lemon tofu](sticky-lemon-garlic-tofu.md) uses 450 g. Still short of the 20 g
bar — raise the tofu to 250 g and it clears; nothing else needs changing.

Claude: this is the leanest tofu recipe here — 1 tsp of oil, no batter, no coating.
Worth knowing when the [orange tofu](crispy-orange-tofu.md) feels too heavy.

Claude: the source names a specific pan brand it was sponsored to feature. Any frying
pan works.
