**Source:** [Healthy Paneer Tikka, Deeparth Kitchen](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/d--rsHsl1xI)

## Ingredients

- 250 g paneer
- 1 cup curd
- 1/2 cup besan
- 1 tsp deggi mirch
- Salt to taste
- 1/2 tsp dhaniya powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp green chilli paste
- 1 tsp oil
- 1/2 tsp kasuri methi
- 1 onion
- 1 capsicum

## Instructions

1. Dry-roast the besan in a pan for 2–3 minutes, until it smells nutty. Cool.
2. Whisk the curd smooth, then mix in the roasted besan, deggi mirch, salt, dhaniya
   powder, turmeric, garlic paste, green chilli paste, kasuri methi and oil to make
   a thick marinade.
3. Cube the paneer, onion and capsicum. Fold through the marinade so everything is
   well coated.
4. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
5. Thread onto skewers, or spread in a single layer in the air fryer basket.
6. Air fry at 180°C for 15–25 minutes, until charred at the edges.

## Notes

Claude: only the ingredient list and the air-fryer setting (step 6) came from the
video description. Steps 1–5 are standard paneer tikka method that I supplied — the
description gave no method at all. Worth checking against the video the first time.

Claude: roasting the besan first (step 1) is the step most often skipped and most
worth doing. Raw besan tastes chalky and doesn't cook out in an air fryer the way it
would over coals.

Claude: 1 cup of curd against 1/2 cup besan makes a thick coating marinade, not a
pourable one. If yours is runny, hang the curd first — thin marinade slides off in
the air fryer basket.

Claude: don't crowd the basket at step 5. The pieces need space or they steam and
you lose the char that makes it tikka.

Claude: the source states no serving count. I've assumed 2, which is your household
and a fair main-course portion at 125 g paneer each — that puts it around 35 g
protein per serving. Served as a starter for 3–4 it wouldn't clear the
`high-protein` bar.

Claude: 15–25 minutes is a wide range because air fryers vary. Start checking at 15.
