## Ingredients

Serves 4.

### Sweet potatoes
- 3 large sweet potatoes, sliced into rounds
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

### Whipped feta
- 1 cup (150 g) feta
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) Greek yogurt or cream
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) lemon juice

### Hot honey
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) honey
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes

### Topping and garnish
- 1/2 cup (60 g) pecans, toasted
- Fresh thyme or parsley
- Cracked black pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F / 200°C.
2. Spread the sweet potato rounds on a baking tray, drizzle with olive oil, salt and
   pepper, and toss to coat.
3. Roast 25–30 minutes, until tender and caramelised at the edges.
4. Blend the feta with the yogurt (or cream) and lemon juice until smooth and fluffy.
5. Warm the honey slightly and stir in the chilli flakes.
6. Spread the whipped feta on a serving plate, or lightly over the roasted sweet
   potatoes.
7. Drizzle over the hot honey.
8. Scatter the toasted pecans, garnish with herbs and cracked black pepper, and serve
   warm.
