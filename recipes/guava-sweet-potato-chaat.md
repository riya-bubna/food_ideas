## Ingredients

One bowl — the source's figures are for the whole recipe.

- 160 g guava, chopped
- 80 g boiled sweet potato, chopped
- Fresh pudina (mint) leaves, chopped — amount not specified
- Green chilli, chopped — amount not specified
- 1 tbsp green chutney
- 2 tbsp stevia-sweetened tamarind chutney
- 1 tsp kooti laal mirch (crushed red chilli)
- 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp crushed peanuts
- Juice of 1 lemon

## Instructions

1. Add the chopped guava and boiled sweet potato to a bowl.
2. Add the chopped pudina leaves and green chilli.
3. Add the green chutney and tamarind chutney.
4. Add the kooti laal mirch, cumin powder, salt and crushed peanuts.
5. Squeeze in the lemon juice.
6. Mix everything well, gently mashing the sweet potato as you mix to create a
   chaat-like texture.

## Notes

Claude: rung 1 — the full recipe, method included, was in the post caption, read
through the browser. Nothing here is reconstructed.

Claude: the recipe is written for one bowl. For the two of you, double everything:
320 g guava (about 2 medium) and 160 g sweet potato (one small).

Claude: the sweet potato has to be boiled first, and the source doesn't count that.
A small one, pricked and wrapped in a damp towel, cooks in the microwave in about
5–6 minutes — no need to boil a pot for 80 g. The 25 minutes above allows for
cooking and cooling it.

Claude: the stevia-sweetened tamarind chutney is the creator's low-calorie choice,
not something the dish depends on. Ordinary saunth works the same way and is
sweeter, so start with 1 tbsp and taste. The source gives no recipe for either
chutney.

Claude: mashing the sweet potato at step 6 is what makes this chaat rather than
fruit salad — it turns into the binder that carries the chutneys onto the guava.
Leave the guava in chunks.

Claude: the source describes this as a high-fibre snack and gives about 7 g protein
for the bowl, which matches the ingredients. It's a snack, not a meal.

Claude: this is the second guava recipe here, after the
[smoky guava dip](smoky-guava-dip.md). Same mint, lemon and chaat seasoning — that
one chars and blends the guava, this one keeps it raw and chunky.
