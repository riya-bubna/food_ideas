## Ingredients

- 50 g paneer
- 5–6 garlic cloves
- 2–3 tbsp milk
- 8–10 basil leaves
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Blend the paneer with the garlic, milk, salt and basil leaves until smooth.
