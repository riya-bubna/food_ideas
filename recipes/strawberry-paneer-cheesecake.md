## Ingredients

Makes 1 pot.

### Base
- 4–5 roasted almonds
- 2 tbsp roasted rolled oats
- 2 tsp ghee
- 2–3 dates
- Pinch of salt

### Cream layer
- 100 g low-fat homemade paneer
- 1–2 tbsp Greek yogurt
- 1–2 tbsp honey or stevia
- 1 tsp vanilla essence
- 2–3 tbsp strawberry compote

### Topping
- 2 tbsp strawberry compote
- 2–3 fresh strawberries, sliced

### Strawberry compote
- 8–10 strawberries, chopped
- 2 tbsp water
- 1 tsp stevia

## Instructions

1. For the compote, put the chopped strawberries in a pan with the water. Cook on a
   low flame until mushy and releasing juice. Mash with a spatula, add the stevia, let
   it thicken slightly, then take off the heat.
2. For the base, grind the almonds, oats, ghee, dates and salt together until well
   combined. Press firmly into your container and refrigerate 10 minutes.
3. Blend the paneer with the Greek yogurt, honey and vanilla until it resembles cream
   cheese. How much yogurt you need depends on how soft or hard your paneer is.
4. Stir 2–3 tbsp of the compote through the blended paneer and pour over the base.
5. Top with more compote and the fresh strawberries, then refrigerate overnight.
