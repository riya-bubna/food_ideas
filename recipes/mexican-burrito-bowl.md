## Ingredients

No quantities are stated anywhere — see the notes. Amounts are yours to judge.

### Rajma base
- Garlic
- Mixed vegetables, whatever is in the fridge (the video shows broccoli, carrots
  and bell peppers)
- Sweet corn
- Salt
- Tomato puree
- Boiled rajma
- Herbs

### Rice
- Cooked rice
- Coriander

### Protein
- Paneer, cubed
- Salt, pepper and seasoning

### To layer
- Avocado, for guacamole (optional)
- Grated cheese

## Instructions

1. Cook the garlic with the mixed vegetables and sweet corn.
2. Add salt and tomato puree. Cook the puree really well.
3. Add the boiled rajma and the herbs, and cook it through properly.
4. Stir rice and coriander into the mix.
5. Separately, grill the paneer cubes with salt, pepper and seasoning.
6. Begin layering the bowl.
7. If you have avocado, make guacamole — it adds an extra layer of texture.
8. Add grated cheese, then another layer of rice.
9. Invert onto a plate to serve.
