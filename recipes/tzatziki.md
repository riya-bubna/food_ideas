Completes the Mediterranean bowl with
[Falafel](falafel.md) and couscous.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup grated cucumber, squeezed
- 1 cup thick Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1–3 garlic cloves, grated
- Salt to taste
- 3 tbsp finely chopped herbs (dill, mint)

## Instructions

The source lists ingredients only and gives no written method.

1. Grate the cucumber and squeeze out as much water as you can.
2. Stir it through the Greek yogurt with the lemon juice, olive oil, grated garlic,
   herbs and salt.
3. Chill before serving.

## Notes

Claude: steps above are the standard method — the caption gave no instructions, only
the ingredient list. Nothing complicated is being assumed, but it isn't the
creator's wording.

Claude: squeezing the cucumber is the entire difference between tzatziki and thin
cucumber raita. Grate it, salt it, leave it 10 minutes, then wring it out in a cloth.
A commenter on the post made exactly this comparison.

Claude: the garlic range is 1–3 cloves, and raw grated garlic gets sharper as it
sits. Start at one if you're making it ahead.

Claude: it thickens and the flavours settle after an hour or two in the fridge, so
make it before the falafel rather than after.

Claude: about 6 g protein per serving — enough that it lifts the falafel bowl over
the high-protein line even though neither component clears it alone.
