## Ingredients

### Fritters
- 300 g frozen peas, cooked
- 1 garlic clove
- A few tbsp nutritional yeast
- A bunch of fresh parsley
- A small bunch of mint
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup white flour (more if the mix is too wet)
- 1/4 cup water (more if too dry)

### Dip
No quantities given for the dip in the source.

- Yoghurt
- Lemon juice
- Mint and/or parsley
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Blend or mash half the peas with the garlic, nutritional yeast and water.
2. Add the remaining peas, the finely chopped herbs, flour, salt and pepper. Mix
   until combined.
3. Shape into small patties.
4. Fry or bake until crispy.
5. Stir the dip together — yoghurt, lemon juice, chopped herbs and salt — and
   serve alongside.

## Notes

Claude: leaving half the peas whole at step 2 is the point of the method. Blending
everything gives you a smooth green patty with no texture.

Claude: the flour and water quantities are explicitly elastic in the source — the
mix should hold a shape without being stiff. Adjust rather than treating 1/2 cup
and 1/4 cup as fixed.

Claude: the fritters themselves are vegan; only the yoghurt dip isn't. Swap in a
plant yoghurt and the whole dish is vegan.

Claude: nutritional yeast is doing the savoury, cheesy heavy lifting here. Without
it these taste flat — it isn't an optional health addition.

Claude: around 10 g protein per serving. The caption pitches peas as protein-rich
and the comments are full of people asking for macros, but at this quantity it's a
light snack, not a protein dish.
