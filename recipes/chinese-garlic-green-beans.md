## Ingredients

Serves 4.

- 1 lb green beans, rinsed and dried
- 1/2 cup neutral oil — 1/4 cup for shallow frying, 2 1/2 tbsp for air frying
- 2 1/2 tbsp minced garlic (about 5–6 cloves)
- 1/4 tsp garlic salt or kosher salt

### Optional
- Chilli crisp or chilli oil
- Red pepper flakes
- Toasted sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Snap the tough ends off the beans, and halve them if very long.
2. Heat the oil in a wok over medium-high. To test it, stand a bamboo chopstick in
   the oil — tiny bubbles around it mean it's ready.
3. Working in batches, fry the beans about 2 minutes, until the skins wrinkle. Lift
   out with a spider strainer onto a paper-towel-lined plate. Repeat.
4. Pour the used oil into a bowl to cool.
5. Reheat the wok over medium-high. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds to a minute,
   until fragrant. Return the beans with the salt, stir and cook 1 minute.
6. Serve, garnished with red pepper flakes and sesame seeds, or chilli crisp.

## Notes

Me: uses the same method on broccoli, finished with yogurt, seeds and feta rather
than the Chinese garnishes.

Claude: the oil quantity scales with method and the caption gives all three — 1/2 cup
for the wok, 1/4 cup shallow, **2 1/2 tbsp for an air fryer**. Given how much you air
fry, that last figure is the useful one and it changes the dish from deep-fried to
barely-oiled.

Claude: the beans must be genuinely dry before step 3. Wet beans in hot oil spit
badly, and the wrinkled skin that gives this its texture won't form.

Claude: the garlic goes into a *reheated, mostly empty* wok, not into the frying oil.
Thirty seconds is all it takes, and garlic burnt at that quantity will ruin the dish.

Claude: the creator's point is that this rescues older, tougher beans with bulging
seeds — the fry tenderises them where steaming or boiling wouldn't. Worth reaching
for when your beans aren't young.

Claude: your broccoli version needs the florets very dry too, and it will take longer
than 2 minutes — broccoli is denser. Yogurt and feta push it Mediterranean, so drop
the sesame and chilli crisp when you go that way.

Claude: compare [beans poriyal](beans-poriyal.md) — the same vegetable treated
completely differently. That one steams the beans in a covered pan and finishes with
coconut; this one fries them hard for texture. Useful to have both.
