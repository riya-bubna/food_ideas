## Ingredients

The video gives no quantities. Amounts are unstated except the paneer, which is
my own.

### Sauce
- Soy sauce
- Honey
- Sesame oil
- Water
- Garlic
- Cornflour

### Stir fry
- French beans
- Mangoes, ripe but firm
- Red bell pepper
- Yellow bell pepper
- 200 g paneer (my amount)
- Red chilli powder
- Garlic
- Ginger

### To garnish
- Sesame seeds
- Spring onion greens

## Instructions

1. Whisk the sauce together — soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, water, garlic and
   cornflour — until smooth. Set aside.
2. Blanch the french beans: plunge into hot water, then straight into ice water.
3. Peel and cube the mangoes.
4. Chop the paneer and cook it with a little red chilli powder until golden. Set
   aside.
5. Over high heat, cook the garlic and ginger.
6. Add the blanched beans and the bell peppers.
7. Add the mangoes.
8. Return the paneer, pour in the sauce, and let it thicken.
9. Garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion greens.

## Notes

Me: method transcribed from the video myself. The caption contained no recipe
at all — it was gated behind commenting "mango" for a DM — so these steps come
from watching, not from the creator's written text.

Claude: with the 200 g of paneer across 3 servings this works out around 14 g
protein and under 300 kcal per serving — so `light` holds, but it does not clear
the `high-protein` bar. Doubling the paneer to 400 g would get it close. All other
quantities remain unstated in the source.

Claude: whisk the cornflour into cold liquid at step 1, never into the hot wok.
Added dry at step 8 it will seize into lumps.

Claude: use mangoes that are ripe but still firm. Soft ripe ones collapse into the
sauce at step 7 and you lose the distinct cubes shown in the video.

Claude: the mango goes in late and barely cooks. Its job is sweetness and bite
against the salty sauce, so resist stirring it for more than a minute.

Claude: honey plus cornflour will catch and burn on a hot wok if the sauce sits.
Have everything else finished before it goes in.
