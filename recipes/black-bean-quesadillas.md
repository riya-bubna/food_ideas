## Ingredients

Makes 4 quesadillas.

### Cashew queso
- 1/3 cup (43 g) cashews
- 2 tbsp (30 g) chipotle in adobo sauce
- 1/4 cup (30 g) nutritional yeast
- 2/3 cup (150 ml) soy milk

### Filling
- 1 tsp (5 ml) olive oil, or water-fry with 1 tbsp water
- 1.5 cups (250 g) cooked black beans
- 1/2 cup (80 g) frozen corn
- 1 red bell pepper, deseeded and diced
- 1 red onion, finely diced
- 2 cups (60 g) baby spinach
- 4 large whole grain tortillas

### Spices
- 1 tsp (2 g) ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) sweet paprika
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) dried oregano
- 1 pinch sea salt flakes

## Instructions

1. Soak the cashews in boiling water for 30 minutes. Drain, then blend with the
   chipotle in adobo, nutritional yeast and soy milk until smooth.
2. In a pan over medium heat, cook the onion in the olive oil for 3–5 minutes, until
   softened.
3. Add the bell pepper and cook 2 minutes. Add the spices, corn and black beans. Stir
   and cook 2–3 minutes.
4. Spread cashew queso over half a tortilla, add the bean mixture and the spinach, and
   fold in half.
5. Transfer to a pan over medium-low heat. Brush with olive oil if needed. Cook 2–3
   minutes until golden, then flip and cook the other side.
6. Repeat with the remaining tortillas. Serve with your favourite toppings.

## Source notes

Published nutrition per quesadilla: 430 kcal, 20 g protein, 51 g carbs, 13 g fat,
13 g fibre, 431 mg sodium.

The creator's own tips:

- Check the tortilla's first ingredient — if it says wholemeal flour, it's a good one.
- Sodium varies a lot between tortilla brands; check the label.
- The filling and the queso both keep refrigerated up to 3 days.

Claude: three things you'll likely need to order — **nutritional yeast**, **chipotle in
adobo**, and whole grain tortillas. The nutritional yeast is what makes the queso taste
cheesy rather than just like blended cashews; there's no substitute for it. Chipotle in
adobo can be approximated with smoked paprika plus a chilli in vinegar, but you lose the
smoke depth. Soy milk is interchangeable with any unsweetened plant milk.

Claude: **the cashew queso is the component worth extracting.** It's a dairy-free cheese
sauce that would work well beyond this recipe — spooned over the

[crispy bean & cheese tacos](crispy-bean-cheese-tacos.md) instead of the mozzarella,

Claude: this is close to the [crispy bean & cheese tacos](crispy-bean-cheese-tacos.md) in

Claude: don't overfill at step 4, and keep the heat at medium-low in step 5. A wet
