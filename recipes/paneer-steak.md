## Ingredients

### Paneer steak
- 2 thick slices paneer (about 150–200 g each)
- 1/2 cup grated boiled sweet potato
- 2 tbsp grated processed cheese
- 2 tbsp boiled sweet corn
- 2 tbsp finely chopped carrots
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- Salt, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp oil, for pan-searing

### Creamy tomato-basil sauce (I skip this)
- 2 medium tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 1 medium onion, roughly chopped
- 2–3 green chillies, slit
- 1 inch ginger, chopped
- 3–4 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt, pepper, chilli flakes, oregano, to taste
- 1/2 tsp sugar, to balance acidity
- 2 tbsp yogurt (hung curd or Greek yogurt preferred)
- A few fresh basil leaves

### Garnish
- Basil leaves
- Sour cream or a dollop of Greek yogurt

## Instructions

1. Combine the sweet potato, cheese, sweet corn, carrots, parsley, flour, lemon
   juice, salt, pepper, chilli flakes and oregano. Mix to a sticky mixture.
2. Pat the paneer steaks dry. Spread a thick, even layer of the mixture over one
   side of each.
3. Heat oil in a non-stick pan. Place the paneer mixture-side down and cook until
   golden and slightly crisp.
4. Gently flip and cook the other side 1–2 minutes, until the paneer takes light
   colour. Remove and set aside.
5. *For the sauce:* heat oil and sauté the tomatoes, onion, green chillies, ginger
   and garlic with salt, pepper, oregano, chilli flakes and sugar. Cook until the
   tomatoes are soft and pulpy.
6. Cool slightly, then blend with the yogurt and basil to a smooth, creamy sauce.
7. Spread the sauce on the plate, set the steak on top, and garnish with sour
   cream and basil.

## Notes

Me: likes the dish but not the tomato-basil sauce. Steps 5–7 are the sauce —
skip them and serve the steak on its own or with something else.

Claude: without the sauce this needs a partner, since the crust is rich but dry.
Two things already in your collection would work — the
[muhammara](muhammara-dip.md), whose smoky-sweet pepper cuts the cheese well, or
the [creamy paneer basil dip](creamy-paneer-basil-dip.md) if you want to keep the
basil note the original was after. A plain herbed yogurt would also do it.

Claude: pat the paneer genuinely dry at step 2. The crust is held on by nothing but
the sticky mixture, and any surface moisture makes it slide off in the pan.

Claude: don't move the steak once it's down at step 3. The crust needs to set
undisturbed or it breaks up when you flip.

Claude: about 40 g protein per serving from 150–200 g of paneer each. Solidly
high-protein even without the sauce.

Claude: the crust mixture is essentially a vegetable-and-cheese bind. It can be
made a few hours ahead and refrigerated, which makes it firmer and easier to
spread.
