Served over [Veg Fried Rice](veg-fried-rice.md) in the video.

## Ingredients

The video labels every ingredient on screen but gives **no quantities**. See the notes
for amounts from the same cook's closely related recipe.

### Paneer marinade
- Paneer, cubed
- Salt
- Crushed black pepper
- Kashmiri red chilli powder
- Ginger garlic paste
- Corn flour

### Sauce
- Oil
- Chopped garlic
- Chopped onion
- Green chilli
- Chopped yellow and red bell pepper
- Salt
- Crushed black pepper
- Kashmiri chilli paste
- Water
- Tomato ketchup
- Soy sauce
- Vinegar
- Corn flour + water, as a slurry

### To finish
- Chopped spring onion

## Instructions

1. Toss the paneer cubes with salt, crushed black pepper, Kashmiri red chilli powder
   and ginger garlic paste.
2. Add corn flour and turn until every cube is coated.
3. Shallow-fry the paneer until golden on all sides. Lift out and set aside.
4. In a wok, heat oil and cook the chopped garlic briefly.
5. Add the onion, green chilli and bell peppers with salt and crushed black pepper.
   Stir-fry on high so they stay crunchy.
6. Add the Kashmiri chilli paste and cook it through.
7. Add water, then the tomato ketchup, soy sauce and vinegar.
8. Stir in the corn flour slurry and cook until the sauce thickens and turns glossy.
9. Return the fried paneer and toss to coat.
10. Finish with chopped spring onion.

## Notes

Claude: recovered by extracting video frames — the short has no description and the
audio is only music. Every ingredient is labelled on screen, but no amount is, so the
step wording is mine.

Claude: **the same cook publishes amounts for a near-identical dish** in the
description of her [fried rice video](https://youtu.be/HWtD8_NaFJk), as "Garlic Hot &
Spicy Paneer": 200 g paneer, 1 tbsp each finely chopped ginger and garlic, 1 onion,
three bell peppers, 1 tsp green chilli, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp crushed black pepper,
1 tsp vinegar, 1 tsp soy sauce, 2 tsp chilli sauce, 2 tbsp tomato ketchup, 1 tsp corn
flour to 3 tsp water. That version also adds garam masala and 1 tsp peanut butter,
which this one doesn't — so use it as a guide to proportions, not as this recipe.

Claude: two separate corn flour steps and they do different jobs — coating the paneer
at step 2 for crispness, and a slurry at step 8 to thicken the sauce. Don't conflate
them.

Claude: fry the paneer and set it aside. Returning it only at step 9 is what keeps the
coating from dissolving into the sauce.

Claude: this pairs with your [fried rice](veg-fried-rice.md) exactly as the cook serves
it, and between them you have a full Indo-Chinese meal — the rice has almost no protein
and this carries it.

Claude: for a lighter version, the [air-fryer paneer tikka](air-fryer-paneer-tikka.md)
method would work on the marinated cubes instead of shallow-frying at step 3.
