## Ingredients

### Chickpea mash
- 1½ cups (245 g) cooked chickpeas
- ½ bell pepper, minced
- ¼ small red onion, minced
- 1 stalk green onion, chopped
- ⅓ cup (80 g) vegan mayonnaise, plus more to taste
- ¼ cup (36 g) chopped pickles (or green olives)
- 3 tbsp (27 g) roasted sunflower seeds
- 2 tbsp (2 g) fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp (2 g) fresh chives, chopped (optional)
- ½ lemon, juiced (optional)
- Salt and ground black pepper, to taste

### Sandwich assembly
- 6 slices multigrain bread (or large whole wheat tortillas, or gluten-free bread)
- 1 medium tomato, sliced
- 1 avocado
- 6 butter lettuce leaves (or spinach, arugula, or any lettuce)
- ¼ cup (50 g) pickled red onions (or pickled jalapeños or pickles)

## Instructions

1. Mash the chickpeas in a large bowl until flaky.
2. Add the remaining mash ingredients and mix well.
3. Spread on bread or a wrap with the fillings — or serve the mash as a snack with
   crackers or vegetables.

**Storage:** assembled sandwiches are best eaten the same day. The mash keeps in an
airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 days. Per the Pick Up Limes team in the
comments, it doesn't freeze well.

## Notes

Claude: extracted from the recipe page (rung 1). The page shows no serving count;
"3 sandwiches" is inferred from the 6 slices of bread.

Claude: 245 g cooked chickpeas is roughly 100–120 g dry. Soak overnight and pressure
cook, which is what turns a 10-minute recipe into a planned one. Cook a bigger batch
and the extra keeps for the next few days.

Claude: if you use the cucumber swap, squeeze the chopped cucumber dry first, the same
as for [tzatziki](tzatziki.md). Hung curd is already wetter than mayo, and the extra
water would make the mash soggy and soak into the bread. That's also a good reason to
start at ¼ cup of curd and only add more if it needs it.

Claude: it comes to about 17–18 g protein per sandwich, just under the 20 g bar.
Blending 50 g of paneer into the hung curd before stirring it through lifts it to
about 20 g without changing the dish much.

Claude: it's the same chickpea base as the [Falafel Smash Wrap](falafel-smash-wrap.md),
so the mash also works as a no-fry filling for those pitas.
