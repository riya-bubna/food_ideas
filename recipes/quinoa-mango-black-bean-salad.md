> **Me: I swap the fruit freely** — grilled pineapple, mango, orange, pomegranate,
> whatever is on hand. Suggest it as a template, not a fixed recipe.

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 1/2 cup (100 g) quinoa
- 1 cup (240 g) water + 1/2 tsp salt, or vegetable broth
- 2 ripe mangoes, diced
- 1 cup (150 g) cucumber, diced
- 1 can (240 g) black beans, or 1 1/2 cups cooked
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 ripe avocado

### Cilantro lime dressing
- 1 cup (25 g) fresh cilantro (parsley works if you don't like cilantro)
- 1/4 cup (55 g) extra virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup (60 g) lime juice (lemon juice if using parsley)
- 2 tbsp (30 g) maple syrup
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Rinse the quinoa under cold water. Add to a small pot with lightly salted water
   or vegetable broth, bring to a boil, lower the heat and simmer until the water
   evaporates — about 12 minutes.
2. Let the quinoa cool before it goes near the vegetables.
3. Blend all the dressing ingredients together.
4. In a large bowl combine the cooled quinoa, red onion, cucumber, red bell pepper,
   mango, drained and rinsed black beans, and diced avocado.
5. Pour over the dressing and toss until well combined.

## Notes

Me: treats the fruit as interchangeable — grilled pineapple, mango, orange or
pomegranate depending on what's around.

Claude: step 2 is not optional. Warm quinoa wilts the cucumber and turns the avocado
to mush, and there's no recovering the texture after that.

Claude: add the avocado last and toss gently, or it breaks up and greys the whole
bowl. If you're making this ahead, hold the avocado back until serving.

Claude: the dressing is where the sweetness lives — 2 tbsp maple against 1/4 cup
lime. If your fruit is very sweet already (pineapple, ripe mango), pull the maple
back or the whole bowl goes cloying.

Claude: about 7 g protein per serving. Quinoa and black beans read as protein-rich,
but spread across four servings with this much fruit it's a fiber dish, not a
protein one.

Claude: the dressing keeps about a week separately, so it's worth making a full
batch even when you're only dressing enough salad for two.
