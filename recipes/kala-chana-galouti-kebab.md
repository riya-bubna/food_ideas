## Ingredients

Makes 10 kebabs.

### To pressure cook
- 1.5 cups black chickpea / kala chana, soaked overnight
- 1 inch ginger, thinly sliced
- Salt to taste
- Water as required

### To blend
- The cooked kala chana
- 1/4 cup curd

### To mix in
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1/2 cup coriander leaves
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1/4 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp jeera powder
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp chaat masala
- 1/4 tsp elaichi powder

### To cook
- 1–2 tbsp ghee

## Instructions

1. Pressure cook the soaked kala chana with the sliced ginger, salt and water for
   5–6 whistles.
2. Blend the cooked chana with the curd to a paste.
3. Mix the paste with the onion, green chilli, coriander leaves, ginger garlic
   paste and all the spices.
4. Shape into tikkis.
5. Pan-fry in ghee for 2–3 minutes per side, until golden.
6. Serve hot with green chutney.

## Notes

Claude: drain the cooked chana well before blending — carrying the cooking water
into step 2 gives a slack mix that won't hold its shape when fried. If the tikkis
still won't bind, chill the mix for 20 minutes rather than adding flour, which
dulls the galouti texture.

Claude: elaichi powder in a savoury kebab is what makes this read as galouti
rather than as a generic chana tikki. Don't skip it, and don't increase it.

Claude: the mix keeps 2 days refrigerated and the shaped raw tikkis freeze well —
worth doubling given the overnight soak.

Claude: around 15 g protein per serving at 4 servings, so it doesn't clear the bar
I use for `high-protein` despite being chana-based. Filling enough as a snack,
light as a main.
