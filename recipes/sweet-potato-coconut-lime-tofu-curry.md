## Ingredients

Serves 2.

### For the tofu
- 400 g (14 oz) extra firm tofu, drained and pressed
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 heaping tsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- Cornstarch, as needed
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### For the spicy sauce
- 4 spring onions (scallions), diced
- 1/2 thumb-sized piece ginger, grated
- 2–3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 tbsp red Thai curry paste
- 2–3 button mushrooms, sliced
- 1 small sweet potato, chopped
- 400 ml (13.5 fl oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 200 ml (6.8 fl oz) unsweetened, unflavoured plant-based milk (pea milk)
- Juice of 1 lime

### To serve
- White or brown rice
- Sliced chilli
- Fresh coriander (cilantro)
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Tear the pressed tofu into rough, bite-sized chunks into a bowl. Tearing gives more
   craggy edges to crisp than cutting does.
2. Pour over the sesame oil, then the miso and maple syrup (whisked together). Dust
   with cornstarch, season with salt and pepper, and toss until coated.
3. Spread the tofu on a lined baking tray. Peel and chop the sweet potato into chunks
   and add them to the same tray.
4. Roast until the tofu is golden and crisp and the sweet potato is tender. *(No
   temperature or time given. See notes.)*
5. Meanwhile, heat a little oil in a wide pan and soften the white parts of the spring
   onions.
6. Add the curry paste, then the mushrooms (along with the ginger and garlic), and
   fry until the paste darkens and the mushrooms soften.
7. Pour in the coconut milk and plant milk and bring to a simmer.
8. Add the roasted tofu and sweet potato and stir through the sauce. Finish with the
   lime juice.
9. Serve over rice, topped with sliced chilli, coriander and sesame seeds.
