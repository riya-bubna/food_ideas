## Ingredients

Cooked by feel; no quantities recorded.

- Goat cheese, whipped
- Pear, sliced
- Cinnamon powder
- Honey
- Pistachios, crushed
- Bread, toasted

## Instructions

1. Whip the goat cheese until light and spreadable.
2. Slice the pear and dust the slices with cinnamon powder.
3. Grill the pear slices until softened and caramelised at the edges.
4. Toast the bread.
5. Spread the whipped goat cheese over the toast.
6. Layer the grilled pear slices on top.
7. Drizzle with honey and scatter over crushed pistachios.
