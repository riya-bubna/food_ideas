## Ingredients

### Baby corn
- 1 can baby corn nuggets
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- Salt, as needed
- Water, as needed

### Sauce
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp spring onions
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp garlic
- 2 tbsp tomato sauce
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp red chilli sauce
- Salt to taste
- 1/2 tsp vinegar
- A pinch of sugar
- 1/2 tsp cornstarch, diluted with water into a slurry

### To garnish
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Drain the baby corn. Toss in a bowl with the flour and salt, adding water a
   little at a time, until every piece is coated in a thick batter.
2. Lay the pieces on foil in the air fryer basket in a single layer.
3. Air fry at 350°F for 8 minutes, then flip and cook 3 minutes more.
4. Heat the oil in a pan. Add the spring onions, then the ginger and garlic, and
   cook briefly.
5. Add the tomato sauce, soy sauce and red chilli sauce. Stir and let it come
   together.
6. Season with salt, the vinegar and a pinch of sugar.
7. Pour in the cornstarch slurry and cook until the sauce thickens and turns glossy.
8. Add the air-fried baby corn and toss until every piece is coated.
9. Garnish with sesame seeds and serve.
