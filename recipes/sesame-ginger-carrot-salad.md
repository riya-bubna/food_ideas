**Source:** [Sesame Ginger Carrot Salad — Allie Petersen, Naturallie Plant Based](https://naturallieplantbased.com/sesame-ginger-carrot-salad/)

Written for 4. Cooking for 2, halve everything — 2 large carrots, 1/2 mango,
1/3 cup edamame, and a dressing of 1 tbsp soy sauce with 1 1/2 tsp each of sesame
oil, rice vinegar and honey.

## Ingredients

### Salad
- ~4 cups carrot ribbons (about 4–5 large carrots, peeled with a vegetable peeler)
- 1 cup fresh mango, diced (about 1 medium mango)
- 2/3 cup shelled edamame, thawed
- 1–2 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup fresh coriander, roughly chopped
- 1–2 tbsp sesame seeds

### Sesame ginger dressing
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (or coconut aminos for gluten-free)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey (or maple syrup)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1–2 tsp fresh ginger, finely minced
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Peel and discard the rough outer skin of the carrots. Hold each carrot firmly and
   run a vegetable peeler along its length to shave long ribbons, rotating as one side
   flattens. Stop at the narrow core. This should give about 4 cups.
2. Add the mango, edamame, spring onions and coriander to a bowl with the carrot
   ribbons.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, honey,
   garlic, ginger, lime juice, salt and pepper.
4. Pour the dressing over the salad and stir until evenly coated.
5. Taste and adjust — more ginger if you want it sharper. Top with sesame seeds.
   Serve immediately, or refrigerate 30 minutes for the flavours to settle.

## Source notes

From the creator:

- Keeps 3–4 days in an airtight container, and the flavours improve as it sits — a
  good make-ahead salad for meal prep.
- The carrot ribbons soften over time. Store the dressing separately if you want to
  keep the crunch.

## Notes

Claude: this is a **side, not a meal**. The edamame is the only protein and 2/3 cup
split four ways is roughly 4 g a serving. To make it lunch, add 150–200 g of
pan-fried paneer or tofu tossed in a little soy — that takes it to ~20 g a serving
and the dressing already suits both. Same move as the tofu in the
[mango peanut lime salad](mango-peanut-lime-salad.md).

Claude: **vegan** if you use the maple syrup instead of honey — the source is a
plant-based site and lists it first-class, so treat maple as the default here.
**Gluten-free** only with the coconut aminos; ordinary soy sauce has wheat in it.

Claude: dress at the table if you're eating it fresh. Salted dressing on carrot
ribbons is the same collapse you get with cucumber in the
[mango peanut lime salad](mango-peanut-lime-salad.md) — about ten minutes and the
ribbons go limp. The creator's make-ahead note and this are not in conflict: the
make-ahead version is deliberately soft, closer to a slaw.

Claude: no oven, no stove, nothing to cook. A peeler is the only real tool, and
shaving 4–5 carrots by hand is most of the ten minutes.

Claude: if you can't get frozen shelled edamame, green peas are the closest swap for
texture and colour, though blander. Don't substitute a soaked-and-cooked legume here —
rajma or chana are too heavy for ribbons this fine.

Claude: this makes three carrot-forward recipes now, and they're pleasingly
different — this one raw and sharp, the
[carrot & beetroot salad](carrot-beetroot-salad.md) South Indian with coconut and
peanuts, and the [balsamic carrots](grilled-balsamic-carrots.md) cooked and sweet.

Claude: the protein this needs is already filed — the
[miso glazed tofu](miso-glazed-tofu.md), half a batch, laid hot over the cold
ribbons. Both dressings are soy, rice vinegar, sesame oil and ginger, so they agree
rather than compete.
