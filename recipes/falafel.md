Part of her "Double Dose of Chana Bowl" — falafel, hummus and couscous salad.
For my bowl: [Tzatziki](tzatziki.md) + couscous.

## Ingredients

Makes 15 falafel.

- 2 cups dried chickpeas (white chana / chole)
- 1/2 cup onion, roughly chopped
- 5–6 garlic cloves, or to taste
- 1–2 green chillies
- 1/4 cup spring onion greens, roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup fresh coriander, cilantro or parsley
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
- 1 tbsp cumin powder
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder or paprika
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp white sesame seeds
- 2 cups vegetable oil, for shallow frying

## Instructions

1. Soak the dried chickpeas in water for 5–6 hours. Drain into a colander.
2. Add the soaked chickpeas, onion, garlic, green chilli, herbs, spices and salt to
   a food processor. Use the PULSE function and grind without water, keeping the
   texture coarse.
3. Transfer to a mixing bowl, mix in the sesame seeds, and refrigerate 10–15
   minutes.
4. Heat the oil in a frying pan for shallow frying.
5. Shape the mixture into round discs about 1/4 inch thick.
6. Fry in small batches until golden and crisp on both sides. Drain on paper.

## Notes

Claude: the reel shows these **air fried**, but the written recipe on her blog is
shallow fried in 2 cups of oil. If you want the air-fryer version, skip steps 4 and
6 and cook at 190°C for about 15 minutes, flipping halfway, with a light spray of
oil. That setting is mine, not hers.

Claude: chickpeas must be raw-soaked, never boiled or canned — the blog is explicit
about this. Cooked chickpeas turn to paste and the falafel disintegrates.

Claude: pulse, don't blend. A smooth purée gives you dense, gummy falafel; you want
a coarse crumb that still shows individual bits.

Claude: the 10–15 minute chill at step 3 is what lets the discs hold together. Skip
it and they slump in the pan.

Claude: about 20 g protein per serving, right at the bar. With tzatziki alongside the
bowl goes comfortably past it.

Claude: 15 falafel is well beyond two people. The shaped raw discs freeze well, so
portion and freeze the rest rather than halving the batch — the soak is the slow
part either way.
