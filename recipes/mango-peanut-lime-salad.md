**Source:** [@adnansdesipan on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZU11boIilg/)

Written for 2 servings — no scaling needed.

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 2 ripe mangoes, cut into chunks
- 1 large cucumber, in ribbons or sliced
- 100 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 handful fresh mint leaves
- 1/4 cup roasted peanuts, crushed
- 1–2 tsp sesame seeds

### Dressing
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp lime or lemon juice
- 1–1 1/2 tbsp tamari or soy sauce
- 1 small green chilli, finely chopped
- 1 tsp maple syrup or honey
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- Black pepper, to taste
- 2–4 tbsp water, to loosen

## Instructions

1. Roast the peanuts, rub off the skins, and crush gently. Toast the sesame seeds too.
2. Heat a little oil in a pan. Add the tofu cubes and let them turn golden. Add a
   splash of tamari or soy sauce and toss for a minute.
3. Cut the mangoes, make cucumber ribbons, and pluck the mint leaves.
4. Whisk the dressing ingredients together, adding water slowly until pourable.
5. Bring everything together on a platter. Pour over the dressing and finish with more
   peanuts and sesame for crunch.

## Source notes

From the creator:

- Taste the dressing before it goes on the salad, and adjust salt, pepper, lime,
  chilli or sweetness as needed.
- If your peanut butter is already salted, go carefully with the salt.
- Paneer works well in place of the tofu.

## Notes

Claude: the creator's own note that **paneer substitutes for the tofu** is worth
acting on — you keep paneer in the house and it fits the same mango-plus-protein
pattern as your [mango paneer stir fry](mango-paneer-stir-fry.md) and

Claude: dress at the table. Cucumber ribbons collapse under a salted dressing within
about ten minutes, same issue as your [kale salad](kale-fruit-feta-salad.md).

Claude: the dressing thins with water, not more acid. Add it a tablespoon at a time —
peanut butter seizes before it loosens, so it looks wrong before it looks right.

Claude: about 12 g protein per serving from only 100 g tofu plus the peanuts. This is
a light lunch, not a main. Doubling the tofu would make it one.

Claude: a commenter made it without the peanut butter and rated it highly, so the
dressing works as a plain lime-tamari-ginger one if you want it lighter.
