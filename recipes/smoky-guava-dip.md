## Ingredients

The source gives no quantities for any ingredient. Amounts below are as stated.

- 1 ripe guava
- Pudina (mint) — amount not specified
- Dhania (coriander) — amount not specified
- Lemon juice — amount not specified
- Chaat masala — amount not specified
- Black salt — amount not specified

## Instructions

1. Roast a ripe guava directly over an open flame until the skin blisters and
   chars.
2. Peel, de-seed and chop.
3. Blend with pudina, dhania, lemon juice, chaat masala and black salt.

## Notes

Claude: the reel lists no quantities at all, so this is a taste-as-you-go recipe
rather than one to follow exactly. As a starting ratio for one guava: a small
handful each of mint and coriander, juice of half a lemon, 1/2 tsp chaat masala
and 1/4 tsp black salt — then adjust. Treat those as my suggestion, not the
creator's.

Claude: the open-flame char is what makes it "smoky" — roasting in an oven or pan
gives you a sweeter, flatter chutney. Worth doing over a gas burner directly.

Claude: in the original this is spread as the base of the bowl with hot kebabs on
top, rather than served alongside.
