> **Me: I use this as a sandwich filling**, not as a side salad. Suggest it that way.

## Ingredients

Vegetable quantities aren't stated — cut what you need. Only the seasoning amounts
are given.

- Onion, julienned
- Capsicum, julienned
- Carrot, julienned
- Cabbage, shredded fine
- Tomato, deseeded and julienned
- Lemon, juiced
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Black pepper powder, a little
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- Curd, cold
- Coriander leaves, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. Julienne the onion, capsicum, carrot and cabbage — cut everything the way you'd
   cut vegetables for noodles, in fine matchsticks.
2. Cut the seedy middle out of the tomato, then julienne the firm flesh.
3. Combine all the vegetables in a large bowl.
4. Squeeze the lemon over, using all of it.
5. Add the salt, black pepper and chilli flakes.
6. Add the cold curd and the chopped coriander.
7. Mix well and serve cold.
