## Ingredients

Makes 4 patties.

### Patties
- 1 package (235 g) Trader Joe's Organic 3 Grain Tempeh
- 1/2 onion
- 2–3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1.5 cups frozen veggie mix
- 1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp hemp seeds
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tbsp butter
- 3.5 tbsp reduced sodium soy sauce
- 2–3 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp chilli powder

### To finish and serve
- 4 slices reduced fat mild cheddar
- 1 tbsp gochujang mayo
- 4 brioche hamburger buns
- Burger toppings of choice — lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, sauces

## Instructions

**Claude: reconstructed. The source gave no steps.**

1. Steam or simmer the tempeh block for about 10 minutes, then pat dry and cool. This
   pulls out tempeh's raw bitterness.
2. Thaw the frozen veggie mix and **squeeze it dry in a towel**. Chop it coarsely.
3. Finely chop the onion and garlic. Soften them in the olive oil and butter until
   translucent, then cool.
4. Crumble or pulse the tempeh to a coarse mince — not a paste.
5. Combine the tempeh, cooked onion and garlic, squeezed veggie mix, breadcrumbs, hemp
   seeds, cornstarch, soy sauce, sriracha, cumin, coriander and chilli powder. Mix
   until it holds together when pressed.
6. Form 4 patties. Chill at least 20 minutes — they firm up and hold their shape far
   better cooked cold.
7. Pan-fry over medium heat, 4–5 minutes a side, until deeply browned and crusted.
8. Lay a slice of cheddar on each patty in the last minute, cover the pan, and let it
   melt.
9. Toast the buns, spread with the gochujang mayo, and build with your toppings.

## Notes

Claude: **rung 1 of the ladder gave the ingredients; nothing gave the method.** The
creator has no blog and no YouTube — her bio points only to TikTok (`@vatscookin`),
which I couldn't reach. Every quantity above is hers, verbatim. Every step is mine.
Send me the video file and I'll replace the method with what she actually does.

Claude: **the mixture will be wet.** 3.5 tbsp of soy sauce plus frozen vegetables that
release water on thawing is a lot of liquid against 1/2 cup breadcrumbs and 2 tbsp
cornstarch. Step 2 is the one that decides whether these hold — don't skip the
squeezing. If it still won't form, add breadcrumbs a tablespoon at a time.

Claude: 3.5 tbsp soy sauce across 4 patties is heavy even at reduced sodium — roughly
500 mg per patty before the sriracha and gochujang mayo. Start at 2 tbsp and taste the
raw mix; you can always add. This lands in the same salty Indo-Chinese-adjacent register
as several other things here, so it's worth pulling back if you're eating those often.

Claude: **no oven needed** — patties are pan-fried, which suits your kitchen. The air
fryer works too: about 180°C for 12–14 minutes, flipping halfway, though you'll trade
some of the crust that butter in the pan gives you.

Claude: **Trader Joe's 3 Grain Tempeh won't be available to you.** Any plain 200–250 g
tempeh block substitutes directly. Hers has grains mixed in, so if yours is plain soy,
consider 2–3 tbsp of cooked millet, quinoa or rice for the same texture. Hemp seeds and
gochujang are the other two you may need to source — sunflower seeds work for the hemp;
there's no real substitute for gochujang's fermented sweetness.

Claude: **this is the first tempeh recipe here**, and the first genuinely new protein
since the tofu entries. At 26 g per burger it's one of the few non-paneer, non-tofu
high-protein mains, which is the gap it fills.

Claude: the patties freeze well raw — form, layer between parchment, freeze, and cook
from frozen with a couple of extra minutes a side.
