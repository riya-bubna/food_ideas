— Episode 1 of #KuchBhi

Written for 2 servings — no scaling needed.

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 1 cucumber, julienned
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 tsp vinegar
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes, or more

### Paneer
- 200 g paneer
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Schezwan sauce, or more
- 1/2 tsp vinegar
- 4–5 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tsp oil, to cook

### Rice
- 1 tsp oil
- A handful of spring onion greens
- 1 cup rice, steamed

### To assemble
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. Cut the carrot and cucumber into juliennes. Put them in **separate** bowls and
   mix the vinegar, soy sauce, sugar and chilli flakes through. Set aside.
2. Cut the paneer into thick slabs. Mix the light and dark soy sauce, Schezwan
   sauce, vinegar and garlic. Add the paneer and marinate 15–20 minutes.
3. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the marinated paneer on both sides.
4. Heat oil in a pan, add the spring onion greens and rice. Mix well, add 1–2 tbsp
   of the paneer marinade, mix and take off the heat.
5. Plate the rice. Top with the carrots, then the paneer, then the cucumber, then
   another slab of paneer.
6. Spoon over the leftover marinade, sprinkle the sesame seeds, garnish with
   coriander, and serve hot.

## Notes

Claude: the carrot and cucumber are dressed in *separate* bowls, and the recipe is
explicit about it. Cucumber weeps and would turn the carrot limp and watery.

Claude: the marinade does double duty — some goes into the rice at step 4 and the
rest is spooned over at the end. Don't discard it after the paneer comes out.

Claude: about 22 g protein per serving from 200 g paneer, just under the bar I use
for `high-protein`. Same paneer quantity you use in the
[mango stir fry](mango-paneer-stir-fry.md), but split two ways instead of three, so
it goes further here.

Claude: "1 cup rice, steamed" reads as already-cooked rice, same phrasing as the
[paneer burrito bowl](paneer-burrito-bowl.md) that you clarified.

Claude: 15–20 minutes of marinating is the only wait, and the rice and salad can
both be done inside it. Real working time is closer to 25 minutes.
