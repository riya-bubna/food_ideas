## Ingredients

No quantities are given anywhere — see the notes. Ingredients below are what the
video shows.

### Avocado layer
- Avocado
- Salt and pepper
- Lemon juice

### Paneer topping
- Paneer, crumbled
- Red onion, finely chopped
- Green chilli, finely chopped
- Cheese, grated
- Salt, pepper and chilli flakes

### To assemble
- Sourdough loaf, cut into thick slabs
- Quail eggs

## Instructions

1. Halve the avocado, scoop the flesh onto a plate, and mash with a fork.
2. Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon, and mash smooth.
3. In a separate bowl, crumble the paneer and mix with the chopped onion, green
   chilli, grated cheese and seasoning.
4. Slice the sourdough into thick slabs.
5. Spread the mashed avocado thickly over each slice.
6. Spoon the paneer mixture over the avocado and spread to the edges.
7. *Source's step, skipped here:* make small wells in the topping and crack a
   quail egg into each.
8. Air fry until the top is golden and the cheese has melted.
