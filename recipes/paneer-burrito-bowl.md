## Ingredients

### Masala paneer
- 250 g paneer, cubed
- Salt and pepper
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder

### Rajma mix
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup rajma, soaked and boiled
- 1/2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp Tabasco
- 1 tbsp coriander
- 2 tbsp tomato purée

### Lemon rice
- 1 cup cooked rice (i.e. 1 cup measured after cooking)
- 2 tbsp coriander
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp butter
- Salt to taste

### Sour cream
- 1 cup hung curd
- Salt to taste
- Lime juice to taste

### To top
- Shredded mozzarella

## Instructions

1. Dice the paneer into small bite-size pieces. If it's store-bought, soak it in
   hot salted water first.
2. Season with salt, pepper, chilli powder, cumin powder and garlic powder, then
   pan-fry until crisp on all sides.
3. For the rajma, heat oil in a pan and sauté the onion and garlic.
4. Add the rajma with the tomato purée for better flavour.
5. Finish with the chopped bell pepper, coriander, salt, pepper, hot sauce and lime
   juice.
6. Stir the lime juice, chopped coriander, salt and butter through the cooked rice.
7. For the sour cream, whisk the hung curd with salt and lime juice to taste.
8. Assemble: rice flat at the bottom, rajma on one side, paneer on the other, a
   dollop of sour cream on top.
9. Scatter mozzarella over the rajma mix.
10. Garnish with coriander and serve.

## Notes

Claude: soaking store-bought paneer in hot salted water (step 1) is the difference
between rubbery and soft. Don't skip it — 10 minutes is enough.

Claude: the bell pepper goes in at the *end* of the rajma mix, not with the onion.
It's meant to stay crunchy against the soft beans.

Claude: hung curd is thick set curd with the whey drained — if you're starting from
regular curd, strain it through muslin for a few hours. Greek yoghurt substitutes
directly.

Claude: around 50 g protein per serving, from paneer, rajma and hung curd together.
This is the first high-protein recipe in your collection that isn't tofu, and by
some margin the highest in it.

Claude: every component holds separately for 2–3 days, so this is the best
meal-prep candidate here. Assemble only when you're eating, or the rice goes soggy
under the sour cream.

Me: "1 cup rice, cooked" means 1 cup of already-cooked rice — so this makes 2
bowls, which is exactly the right size here. No scaling needed.
