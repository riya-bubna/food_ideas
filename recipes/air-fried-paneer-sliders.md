**Source:** [@salonikukreja on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Db5dqNfILBx/)

> **The mayo is the only non-vegetarian element.** Eggless mayo is the default in most
> Indian stores and works exactly the same — see the notes for a hung curd version that
> also raises the protein.

## Ingredients

Makes 4 sliders.

### Sliders
- Burger buns — 4 nos
- Paneer — 200 g
- Cornstarch — 1 tbsp
- Onion — 1/2 a small, diced
- Capsicum — 1/2, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Sauce
- 1/2 cup mayo
- 2 tbsp sriracha or chilli sauce
- 1/4 tsp chilli powder
- 1/4 tsp jeera powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

### To finish
- Melted butter, to brush
- A blend of cheddar and mozzarella cheese — amount not specified
- Coriander, to garnish

## Instructions

1. On a tray lined with parchment, roughly break the paneer into small pieces. Season
   with salt and pepper, add the cornstarch, and mix with a spoon to form a coating.
2. Drizzle over some oil and air fry at 200°C for 8–10 minutes, until crispy and
   golden brown.
3. On the same tray, add the diced onion, bell pepper and chopped garlic. Season with
   salt and pepper and mix well.
4. Air fry this mixture at 200°C for a further 5 minutes.
5. In a bowl, combine the paneer and vegetables with the mayonnaise, sriracha, chilli
   powder and jeera powder. Mix well.
6. Adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper and combine.
7. Cut the buns vertically and scoop out the centre to make a cavity. Brush the tops
   with melted butter.
8. Fill with the paneer mixture, top with the cheese, and air fry a further 5 minutes
   at 180°C, until the cheese melts and turns lightly golden and crisp.
9. Serve hot, garnished with coriander.

## Notes

Claude: rung 1 — the caption carries the whole recipe, ingredients and method. Every
quantity is hers.

Claude: **this is the first recipe here that is entirely air fryer, start to finish.**
Three stages, one appliance, no hob and no oven. Given your kitchen, it's about as
frictionless as a recipe gets.

Claude: the source gives 4 buns and never states servings. **Two sliders per person is
my read**, which makes this dinner for the two of you at roughly 35 g protein each —
comfortably `high-protein`. One slider alone is about 17 g and doesn't clear the bar.

Claude: **replacing half the mayo with hung curd** is worth doing. It cuts the fat
noticeably, adds a few grams of protein, and the sriracha and jeera carry the flavour
regardless. Going all curd makes the filling looser and less rich — half and half holds
the texture. This is the same move as the [creamy paneer basil dip](creamy-paneer-basil-dip.md).

Claude: **steps 1–2 are the reusable part.** Torn paneer, seasoned, tossed in cornstarch
and air fried at 200°C is a crisp paneer base that stands on its own. It's the same
result the [chilli paneer](chilli-paneer.md) gets by shallow-frying, so use it there and
skip the oil. It would also drop straight into the
[veg fried rice](veg-fried-rice.md), which has almost no protein of its own.

Claude: **tear the paneer, don't cube it.** She says "roughly break", and the ragged
edges are what hold the cornstarch and crisp. Same principle as the tofu recipes here.

Claude: scooping the bun at step 7 isn't optional — without the cavity the filling sits
on top and slides out, and the slider won't close.

Claude: don't skip the second air fry at step 8. It's what melts the cheese into the
filling and re-crisps the bun after the wet mixture goes in.

Claude: the creamy paneer filling works well outside the bun. It's close to a sandwich
filling in the same way you use the
[julienne curd salad](julienne-curd-salad.md), and it would fill the
[falafel smash wrap](falafel-smash-wrap.md) format directly.

Claude: same creator as your [crispy bean & cheese tacos](crispy-bean-cheese-tacos.md) —
and the same underlying idea, a rich filling stuffed into a carb and crisped with cheese.
That one uses the pan; this one uses the air fryer.
