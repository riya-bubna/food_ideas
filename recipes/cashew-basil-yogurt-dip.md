## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup cashews (walnuts work as a substitute)
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1/3 cup cream cheese
- 1/2 cup hung curd, hung at least 1 hour — the thicker the better (hung Greek
  yogurt also works)
- 1 tbsp mint
- 2 tbsp basil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- A pinch of sugar
- Olive oil, to garnish
- Chilli flakes, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Boil the cashews for 4–5 minutes, or soak them overnight.
2. To a blender add the boiled cashews, chopped garlic, mint and basil, lime juice,
   cream cheese, hung curd, a pinch of sugar, and salt and pepper. Blend until
   smooth and lump-free.
3. Transfer to a bowl and garnish with olive oil and chilli flakes.
4. Serve with chips or vegetables, as a dip or a spread.

## Notes

Claude: softening the cashews first is what makes this blend smooth rather than
gritty. The 4–5 minute boil is the shortcut; overnight soaking gives a slightly
silkier result.

Claude: "hung curd" means curd drained of its whey — hang it in muslin for at least
an hour. Using it undrained gives you a thin, splitting dip rather than a thick one.

Claude: the pinch of sugar isn't for sweetness, it's balancing the lime and the sour
curd. Easy to skip and easy to notice missing.

Claude: this and the [paneer basil dip](creamy-paneer-basil-dip.md) are worth
comparing directly — that one is 50 g paneer blended with garlic, milk and basil for
a single sandwich, this one is a richer party-sized dip built on cream cheese and
cashews. Same flavour idea, very different weight.

Claude: about 4 g protein per serving as a dip. Rich rather than substantial.

Claude: commenters asked about shelf life and got replies I couldn't read. Dairy-based
and uncooked, so treat it as a 3–4 day fridge item — that's my estimate, not hers.
