## Ingredients

Serves 4.

### Orange sauce
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tbsp minced ginger
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1–2 tsp red chilli flakes
- 1/4 cup white vinegar
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (unseasoned)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 tsp cornstarch + 2 tsp water
- 1 tsp sesame oil

### Crispy tofu
- 16 oz super-firm tofu
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

### Sauce
1. Heat 1–2 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium for a minute.
2. Add the garlic, ginger and chilli flakes and cook a minute.
3. Add both vinegars, the soy sauce, sugar and orange juice. Simmer 2–3 minutes.
4. Mix the cornstarch with the water and stir it in.
5. Heat a minute to activate the cornstarch and thicken to the consistency you want.
6. Finish with the sesame oil and keep warm on low.

### Tofu
7. Heat a layer of oil in a 12" skillet to 375°F.
8. Break the tofu into bite-sized pieces.
9. Toss the tofu in the soy sauce first, then in the seasonings and cornstarch.
10. Fry 3–5 minutes, flip, and fry another 3–5 minutes, until golden.
11. Toss the fried tofu in the orange sauce to coat.
12. Serve immediately over rice.

## Notes

Claude: soy sauce *first*, then the dry mix (step 9). The liquid is what makes the
cornstarch and seasonings stick — reverse the order and the coating falls off in the
oil.

Claude: **break** the tofu rather than cubing it. Third recipe here to say so, after
the [sticky lemon tofu](sticky-lemon-garlic-tofu.md) and the
[cornmeal tofu](miso-sweet-potato-cornmeal-tofu.md) — torn edges hold coating and
crisp where clean cuts don't. It's clearly the technique that matters with tofu.

Claude: sauce first, tofu second, and toss at the last moment. The coating softens
within a couple of minutes of hitting the sauce.

Claude: 1/4 cup sugar plus 1/2 cup orange juice makes this properly sweet —
takeaway-style rather than the restrained sweetness of the sticky lemon tofu. Pull
the sugar back to 2–3 tbsp if that's not what you want.

Claude: about 30 g protein per serving. Your third high-protein tofu main, and the
only deep-fried thing in the collection.

Claude: the sauce is worth knowing on its own. It would work on the
[air-fryer paneer tikka](air-fryer-paneer-tikka.md) cubes, or on the air-fried
cauliflower-style treatment you use for baby corn.
