A second falafel format alongside
[Falafel](falafel.md).

## Ingredients

Makes 3–4 wraps.

### Falafel
- 1 can (14.5 oz / 410 g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup (20 g) parsley, chopped
- 1/4 cup (10 g) cilantro, chopped
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 tsp salt

### Garlic tahini sauce
- 1/2 cup (120 g) tahini
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) water

### To assemble
- Arabic-style pita bread or tortilla wraps
- Pickles, your choice
- Tomatoes, sliced
- Onion, thinly sliced
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Olive oil, for the pan
- Optional: lettuce or arugula

## Instructions

1. In a food processor, combine the chickpeas, parsley, cilantro, cumin,
   coriander, cinnamon, red pepper flakes, salt and flour. Pulse until well
   combined but still slightly chunky.
2. Transfer to a bowl and refrigerate 30 minutes to firm up.
3. Whisk the tahini, crushed garlic, salt, lemon juice and water into a pourable
   sauce.
4. Heat olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
5. Scoop about 3/4 cup of the falafel mixture onto a pita and press to spread
   evenly across the surface.
6. Place the pita in the skillet falafel-side down. Cook 5–7 minutes, until the
   falafel is browned and the bread crisps at the edges.
7. For extra crispiness, flip and cook the other side 2 more minutes.
8. Top with the garlic tahini sauce, pickles, tomatoes, onion and parsley. Fold
   and eat immediately.

## Notes

Claude: this uses *canned* chickpeas, where the air fryer falafel needs raw-soaked
ones. That's not an inconsistency — smashing the mix onto bread means it never has
to hold its own shape, so cooked chickpeas are fine here. Don't carry the shortcut
back to the other recipe; it will fall apart.

Claude: the tahini sauce isn't given its own step in the source. I've made it
step 3 so it's ready before the skillet work, which moves fast once the pita is
down.

Claude: 1 tsp cinnamon in a savoury falafel is unusual and deliberate — it's what
makes this taste different from a standard falafel. Worth keeping the first time
before deciding whether to cut it.

Claude: don't skip the 30-minute chill at step 2. Warm mixture slides off the pita
instead of pressing into it.

Claude: around 22 g protein per serving, which clears the 20 g bar — the tahini does a
lot of that work.

Claude: eat immediately. This goes soggy within minutes, so it doesn't reheat and
isn't a meal-prep candidate the way the air fryer falafel is.

Claude: for a no-fry version, the [Mashed Chickpea & Dill Sandwich](mashed-chickpea-dill-sandwich.md)
mash goes into the same pita with the same fillings.
