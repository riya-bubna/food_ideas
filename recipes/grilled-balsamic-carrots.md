**Source:** [Balsamic Carrots Glazed on the Grill, Two Kooks In The Kitchen](https://twokooksinthekitchen.com/grilled-carrots-with-balsamic-glaze/)

> **Me: I serve these over a bed of yogurt with crumbled feta and fresh herbs.**
> That base isn't in the original recipe — suggest it my way.

## Ingredients

Serves 8–10 as a side.

### Carrots
- 2 lb whole carrots, peeled (about 30 thin carrots, or 10–15 medium)

### Balsamic glaze
- 3 tbsp good-quality balsamic vinegar
- 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup (or honey, or brown sugar)
- 1/2 tbsp lime juice (or lemon)
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated (or a pinch of ground)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves, chopped

### To serve — my addition
- Thick yogurt
- Feta, crumbled
- Fresh coriander and mint
- Chopped green onion, thyme or chives

## Instructions

1. Heat a gas grill to medium-high (425–450°F) and oil the grates.
2. Cut medium or large carrots lengthwise to about 1/2–3/4 inch thick. Boil in
   salted water for 7 minutes, until tender. Drain.
3. Stir all the glaze ingredients together in a small bowl.
4. Lay the carrots on a baking sheet, brush with glaze, then grill in a single
   layer for 5–6 minutes total, turning and re-glazing several times.
5. **My step:** spread thick yogurt across the base of a shallow bowl.
6. Pile the carrots on the yogurt, drizzle over the remaining glaze, and finish
   with crumbled feta, fresh herbs and sea salt.

## Notes

Me: the yogurt base and feta are mine — the original serves the carrots plain with
herbs. It's what turns a side dish into something closer to a mezze plate.

Claude: par-boiling first (step 2) is what makes this work on a grill. Raw carrots
would char outside and stay hard inside in 5–6 minutes.

Claude: glaze repeatedly rather than all at once. Maple and balsamic both burn, so
thin layers built up over the 5–6 minutes give you lacquer instead of scorch.

Claude: season the yogurt with salt and a squeeze of lemon before spreading it.
Unseasoned yogurt under a sweet-sour glaze reads as flat.

Claude: take the carrots off the heat before they go soft. The contrast against the
cold yogurt is the point, and limp carrots lose it.

Claude: a ridged cast-iron grill pan works indoors — same approach as the
[chimichurri tofu](chimichurri-grilled-tofu.md). Get it properly hot first.

Claude: the glaze keeps well, so it's worth making the full batch even for two.
