## Ingredients

### Chimichurri
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 2–3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 cup flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh oregano, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 Fresno pepper, minced
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

### Tofu and marinade
- 2 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp vegan Worcestershire sauce
- Black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp chimichurri, from above

## Instructions

1. Drain the tofu and cut each block into roughly 1/2" steaks — about 4 slices per
   block, 8 in total.
2. Press the steaks between clean kitchen towels under a flat weight for about
   10 minutes to draw out moisture.
3. Finely mince the parsley, oregano, garlic and Fresno chilli. Mix with the olive
   oil, red wine vinegar, salt and pepper. Set aside.
4. In a shallow dish, whisk together the olive oil, soy sauce, maple syrup,
   Worcestershire sauce and black pepper.
5. Stir 2 tbsp of the chimichurri into the marinade.
6. Layer the tofu steaks in the dish and coat evenly. Cover and refrigerate at
   least 2 hours or overnight, flipping gently after about an hour.
7. Preheat the grill to 400°F, clean the grates and brush lightly with oil.
8. Grill the steaks 5–6 minutes per side, until golden with blackened grill marks.
   Baste with extra marinade after flipping if you like.
9. Spoon the remaining chimichurri over the top to serve.

## Notes

Claude: the marinade time is the whole recipe — plan a day ahead. Active work is
only about 30 minutes.

Claude: pressing (step 2) is not optional. Wet tofu won't take the marinade and
will steam rather than char on the grill.

Claude: a ridged cast-iron grill pan works if you're not firing up an outdoor
grill — get it properly hot before the tofu goes on.

Claude: the chimichurri is raw and keeps about 4–5 days refrigerated. Make the
full amount; the leftovers are good on almost anything.
